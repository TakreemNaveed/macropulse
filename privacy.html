Privacy Policy

Application: Macropulse (Android Package: com.tanaveed.calorietracker)
Effective Date: November 9, 2026 • Last Updated: November 9, 2026

At Macropulse, your privacy and health data security are our top priorities. We believe your nutritional intake, body weight, and health habits belong to you. We do not sell your personal information or dietary logs to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Macropulse ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Takreem Naveed, collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application available on Android and related services.

By downloading, accessing, or using Macropulse, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices outlined in this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide you with personalized calorie and macronutrient calculations, meal logging, and progress tracking:

A. Profile and Fitness Metrics

  • Physical Attributes: Age, biological sex, height, current scale weight, and goal weight.
  • Fitness Goals: Daily target calories, protein, carbohydrate, fat ratios, and activity expenditure level.
  • Hydration: Daily water intake logs.

B. Dietary and Nutrition Records

  • Food entries logged by meal category (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks).
  • Quantities, portion sizes, nutritional breakdowns, and timestamps of logged foods.
  • Custom recipes, favorites, and saved food items.

C. Account & Authentication Information

  • Email/Password Sign-In: If you create a local or cloud account with email, we store your email address and display name. Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed.
  • Google Sign-In: If you choose to authenticate via Google OAuth, we receive basic public profile information permitted by Google: your name, email address, profile picture URL, and a unique Google user identifier. We do not access your Google contacts, emails, or personal Drive files.
  • Guest Mode: You may use Macropulse anonymously without providing any email or social login. In Guest Mode, all data is stored exclusively on your device.

3. Device Permissions & Camera Use

To provide core photo and barcode scanning capabilities, Macropulse requests access to specific device features:

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Used strictly in real-time when you tap the camera viewfinder to photograph a meal plate for nutritional estimation, or scan a standardized food barcode (UPC/EAN).
  • Flashlight: You may toggle the camera torch during scanning to illuminate meals or barcodes in low-light environments.
  • Photo Library Access: Used only when you voluntarily select an existing food image from your device gallery to analyze a meal.
  • No Audio or Eavesdropping: Macropulse does not record audio or access your device microphone.

4. How We Use Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Macropulse incorporates artificial intelligence features (such as the MacroPulse AI Food Bot and Plate Visual Recognition) via the Google Gemini API.

  • When you capture a food photo or submit a natural language description (e.g. "2 slices of sourdough bread with peanut butter"), the data is transmitted securely to the AI API solely to generate nutritional estimates (estimated grams, calories, protein, carbs, and fat).
  • Images and text prompts sent for AI analysis are processed transiently and are never used to train public AI models without consent.

5. Nutritional Database Sources

Macropulse references public and verified nutritional repositories to provide accurate nutritional data:

  • USDA FoodData Central: Public domain nutritional information published by the U.S. Department of Agriculture.
  • Open Food Facts: Open-access global food product database.
  • Desi Food Composition Tables: Regional nutritional tables from the Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT) and Pakistan Food Composition Tables.

6. Data Security and Storage

  • Encryption in Transit: All network requests made by Macropulse (including database queries and API lookups) are transmitted over modern cryptographic protocols using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Device Security: Offline data is stored in localized sandboxed SQLite database structures on your physical device, protected by Android's application isolation sandbox.
  • Integrity Verification: In-app settings and subscriptions utilize cryptographic integrity hashes to prevent tampering or corruption.

7. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information. We share data only with the following technical infrastructure providers necessary to operate the application:

  • Authentication Providers: Google Identity Services (for users choosing Google Sign-In).
  • AI Processing: Google Cloud / Gemini API (for meal plate and text analysis).
  • Nutritional Lookups: USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts API (for barcode queries).

8. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have full sovereignty over your information:

  • Access and Review: You can inspect all historical logs, weights, and food entries directly inside the Diary and Progress tabs.
  • Deletion: You can delete individual food logs, clear your chat history, or delete your entire profile and local database from the in-app Settings screen at any time.
  • Complete Account Deletion: To request permanent deletion of any cloud-associated account data, contact the developer at takreemnaveed@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". All records will be removed within 14 business days.

9. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Macropulse is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete such data from our records.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements to Macropulse or changes in regulatory requirements. When updates occur, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will be revised. Continued use of the application after changes constitute acceptance of the updated policy.
